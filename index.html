Bed bug treatment questions

Is the treatment poisonous? Will the chemicals stay on my things?

Neptune uses eco-friendly, non-toxic products and follows Integrated Pest Management. You step out for 2 to 4 hours while products dry, and the technician briefs you before starting.

How do I prepare?

Strip the beds and bag the bedding. Keep furniture where it is, so bed bugs don’t spread to other rooms. We send you the full checklist before we arrive.

Will you leave my house in a mess?

No. Technicians cover their shoes before walking in, and tidy up after the treatment.
